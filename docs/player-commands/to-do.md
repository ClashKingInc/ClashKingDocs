# 🤔 To-do

Use `/player to-do` in Discord to see outstanding attacks and other tracked activity for linked accounts.

## View a to-do list

Run `/player to-do` without options to check your own visible linked accounts. To check another person's accounts, select them with the optional **discord_user** option. Hidden accounts are excluded. If there are no visible linked accounts, see [Link players](../bot-commands/link-players.md).

The list uses up to 20 visible accounts. When more are available, verified accounts are included first. For example, select a clanmate as **discord_user** to check their visible accounts.

## Understand the results

Sections appear when there are relevant results and data is available:

- **War Hits**: remaining attacks for an account included in a war during preparation or battle, with the time until it ends.
- **Legend Hits** and **Ranked Hits**: remaining attacks for eligible accounts.
- **Raid Hits**: remaining attacks during an active Raid Weekend for eligible accounts.
- **Clan Games**: progress below 4,000 points while Clan Games are active.
- **Season Pass (Top 10)**: up to ten accounts with recorded progress above zero and below 4,000 points.
- **Inactive Accounts (48+ hr)**: accounts last recorded online at least 48 hours ago.
- **Capital Dono (24+ hr)**: accounts whose last recorded Capital donation was at least 24 hours ago.

If data cannot be loaded, the result identifies unavailable sections. Missing data should not be interpreted as a completed task. Use the refresh button to update the list and the navigation buttons when there are multiple pages.
