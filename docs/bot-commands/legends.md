# 🟣 Legends

Use the Legends commands in Discord to view a player's Legend League day and season, a clan's players, global statistics, and past season finishes.

## View a player

Use `/legends player` and select a **player** or enter their tag. The result starts with today's Legend day: recorded attacks and defenses, trophy changes, and the most used army when available. A season graphic is shown when recorded day data is available.

Use **Today**, **Yesterday**, and **Season** below the result to switch views. The season view shows totals, averages, and recorded daily trophy changes. For previous season finishes, use `/legends history` below.

:::info
If Legend statistics look incorrect, check the [FAQ](../faq.md).
:::

## View a clan

Use `/legends clan` and select a **clan** or enter its tag. The result lists the clan's current Legend players, their trophies, today's recorded attacks and defenses, net trophy change, and global rank when available. Use the refresh button to update the result or **Open in app** to view the clan in ClashKing.

## Explore global statistics

Use `/legends stats` and choose a view from **Legend statistics** below the result:

- **Trophy distribution**: the distribution of ranked players across trophy ranges.
- **Rank cutoffs**: trophy counts at selected global ranks.
- **Triple streaks**: consecutive recorded three-star attacks. A ≥ sign means the displayed streak is a lower bound.
- **Season winners**: recorded winners and their finishing trophies.
- **Daily performance**: recent attack statistics for Legend I and the top 1,000 and 200 players.

The optional **limit** accepts a number from 1 to 50 and controls the maximum number of entries in applicable lists; it defaults to 50. Use the result's navigation or refresh controls where available.

## View season history

Use `/legends history` and select a **player** or enter their tag to see recorded Legend season finishes. The optional **limit** selects how many recent finishes to show, from 1 to 240; without it, all available finishes are shown. Use the navigation buttons when the result has multiple pages.
